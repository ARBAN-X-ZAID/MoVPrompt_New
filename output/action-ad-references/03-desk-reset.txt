03 — THE SATISFYING DESK RESET
Reference image: 03-desk-reset.png
Example subject: fictional walnut organizer tray and three everyday objects. Suitable for organizer products with demonstrated physical capacity.

Create an 8-second vertical 9:16 organization-product advertisement. Completely silent. Show the practical transformation from loose belongings to an orderly arrangement through visible physical action.

REFERENCE LOCK:
Use the uploaded organizer and belongings as the source of truth. Preserve the exact tray shape, material, dimensions, rim and compartments. Keep the number and identity of the supplied belongings unchanged. The example contains three movable belongings outside a single open tray. Do not invent storage features, enlarge the tray or shrink objects to make them fit.

0–1 SECOND — SHOW THE NEED:
Begin in the uploaded composition, with loose objects beside the empty organizer. The camera is fixed in an elevated tabletop view. One adult hand enters and reaches for the first object, so the action starts immediately.

1–3 SECONDS — FIRST PLACEMENT:
The hand lifts the first loose belonging nearest the tray with a natural grip and sets it inside. Keep connected parts attached and preserve its shape. Lift over the rim; do not slide through a solid wall.

3–4.5 SECONDS — SECOND PLACEMENT:
The same hand picks up the second supplied belonging and places it neatly along an inside edge. Keep its size and orientation physically consistent and avoid overlapping the first item.

4.5–6.5 SECONDS — FINAL PLACEMENT:
The hand lifts the last supplied belonging using a grip appropriate to its actual shape and lays it gently in the remaining space. Preserve all parts and keep any folded components folded. Do not stretch or duplicate it. The hand withdraws.

6.5–8 SECONDS — CLEAR RESULT:
Hold the same camera position so viewers can compare the now-clear desk area with the organized tray. Show all three objects together inside it. Slight natural background light movement keeps the shot alive without zooming or rotating.

RULES:
No teleporting, spontaneous object movement, disappearing items, extra fingers or hands, forced overlaps, changed product dimensions, split-screen before/after, generated text or unsupported performance claims. Keep lighting and perspective consistent. No audio.

ADAPTING TO A DIFFERENT UPLOAD:
Use the actual supplied belongings and realistic grips. Only use this three-placement recipe when the image contains three movable belongings and the organizer can genuinely hold them. Do not introduce objects absent from the upload.

Optional separately edited CTA: A place for your everyday essentials. Add only verified product and purchase details.
