02 — THE SATISFYING POUR
Reference image: 02-satisfying-pour.png
Example subject: fictional iced coffee preparation. Suitable for drink references showing the actual ingredients and pouring vessel.

Create an 8-second vertical 9:16 cafe advertisement. Completely silent. The main attraction is a real pour and liquid mixing, not camera movement.

REFERENCE LOCK:
Start from the uploaded arrangement. Preserve the exact glass, pitcher, liquid types, ice, surface, proportions and colors. Use only the drink ingredients shown or explicitly confirmed. Do not replace the uploaded drink with another recipe. Liquid level and mixing may change as a direct result of the pour; containers retain their exact design.

0–1.5 SECONDS — ACTION IMMEDIATELY:
With the camera locked at the reference angle, one adult hand enters from the right, grips the pitcher's handle and lifts it toward the glass. Keep both vessels visible and the movement practical rather than theatrical.

1.5–4.5 SECONDS — THE POUR:
Tilt the pitcher and pour one continuous narrow stream into the glass. The dark liquid travels between the existing ice and forms curling brown ribbons through the lighter liquid. Ice shifts slightly from the pour. The fill level rises gradually and remains safely below the rim. No splash explosion or overflow.

4.5–6 SECONDS — SATISFYING RESOLUTION:
The hand rotates the pitcher upright, stopping the stream naturally, and returns it to its original area. Coffee remaining inside the pitcher reflects what was poured out. No duplicate vessel or disappearing liquid. The hand exits.

6–8 SECONDS — APPETIZING FINISH:
Keep the camera stationary while the layered drink continues mixing in delicate swirls. Ice movement subsides naturally. Warm light reveals the glass and existing condensation. The fluid remains subtly active through the final frame.

RULES:
No camera orbit, repeated zoom, new ingredients, extra glassware, generated text, floating pitcher, impossible fluid levels or exaggerated condensation. One consistent hand with a natural grip. No claims about health, flavor or ingredients beyond supplied facts. No audio.

Optional separately edited CTA: Your next coffee break. Use only the actual menu name, price and ordering details if confirmed.
