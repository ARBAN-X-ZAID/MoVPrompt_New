## 1. System Classification

**System Type:** Content Generation

**Description:**
MovPrompt turns a configuration-bound Kuwait campaign project version into a durable, privately stored, technically valid and quality-approved video output. The browser starts and observes the operation, while PostgreSQL, pg-boss workers, the approved Seedance capability adapter, private object storage and deterministic media tooling control submission, recovery, validation and settlement.

**Critical Failure Modes:**
1. Duplicate provider submission, charge, entitlement consumption or refund.
2. Marking an output complete before private copy, full media validation and quality approval.
3. Product, price, offer, CTA, language or Arabic-layout drift from confirmed campaign facts.
4. Losing an accepted provider operation or output when the browser, worker or network restarts.
5. Exposing provider identifiers, credentials, signed URLs or unsafe output hosts.

## 1b. Domain Context

**Industry Vertical:** Kuwait-first social campaign production for retail, ecommerce, salons and clinics.

**User Population:** Business owners and marketing teams with little or no video-production expertise. Templates must protect supplied facts rather than requiring users to spot AI mistakes. Clinic accounts also need an authorized clinical/compliance reviewer before release.

**Stakes Level:** High. A polished but inaccurate campaign can mislead customers, damage a local business's reputation and send orders or bookings to the wrong offer. The stakes are critical for clinic/pharmacy-adjacent content because misleading health claims, unapproved price promotions, or patient exposure create regulatory and patient-privacy harm.

**Output Consequence:** The accepted video is a paid, ready-to-publish social asset. It will be acted on as a representation of the product, offer, booking destination, service or clinic; it must be commercially truthful, legible in the selected language and safe to export—not merely technically playable.

### What Domain Experts Evaluate Against

Dimension: Product and business truth
Good (domain expert would accept): The actual packshot/product, packaging, logo, colour, material, service name, confirmed price, offer, CTA, WhatsApp or booking destination remain unchanged from the immutable brief; creative lighting, setting and camera work do not create a different item or promise.
Bad (domain expert would flag): A bottle label changes, a product gains a feature or different variant, the price/discount/CTA is altered, or a salon/retail service is represented with an unconfirmed result.
Stakes: Critical
Source: Practitioner requirement for catalog and conversion creative; MovPrompt protected-layer and confirmed-fact contract in `packages/creative-engine/src/catalog.ts` and `prompt-compiler.ts`; product-advertising evaluation research.

Dimension: Kuwait Arabic and bilingual reading integrity
Good (domain expert would accept): Arabic is native Kuwait Arabic at the chosen register, joined and readable, with right-to-left copy, left-to-right numbers/KWD/WhatsApp elements, punctuation and English runs in their intended order; bilingual copy is independently readable rather than a duplicated or clipped translation.
Bad (domain expert would flag): Misspelled or disconnected Arabic letters, Egyptian/Levantine/Modern Standard Arabic delivery where Kuwait Arabic was requested, reversed KWD/phone numbers, collided languages, or copy that cannot be read before the scene changes.
Stakes: High
Source: Kuwait campaign-language policy in `packages/creative-engine/src/kuwaiti-arabic.ts`; [W3C Arabic and Persian Layout Requirements](https://www.w3.org/TR/alreq/) on RTL text, numbers and bidirectional runs.

Dimension: Conversion-frame legibility and safe placement
Good (domain expert would accept): The deterministic price, offer, logo, subtitles and CTA have enough clear, stable space in their designated scene; the hero subject does not cover them, and the final booking or WhatsApp action is visible at the requested ratio.
Bad (domain expert would flag): The product, a hand, captions or generated decoration overlaps the CTA/price; edge cropping removes the logo; or a video may look attractive yet leaves the viewer unable to identify the offer or next action.
Stakes: High
Source: Template scene recipes and protected layers in `packages/creative-engine/src/catalog.ts`; accepted-output quality policy in `packages/creative-engine/src/quality-engine.ts`.

Dimension: Credible, publishable motion and people
Good (domain expert would accept): Product handling, hands, reflections, camera movement, speech and any consented speaker remain physically plausible and consistent between shots; a testimonial retains the approved speaker's meaning and identity.
Bad (domain expert would flag): Extra fingers, warped packaging, flicker, identity drift, unsynchronised visible speech, an invented testimonial, or an AI-generated person presented as a real customer without the required consent basis.
Stakes: High
Source: Creative-engine negative prompt, UGC/testimonial requirements and quality dimensions in `packages/creative-engine/src/prompt-compiler.ts`, `catalog.ts` and `quality-engine.ts`.

Dimension: Clinic claim, price and patient-privacy safety
Good (domain expert would accept): A clinic campaign names only confirmed, licensed services/people and approved facts; it contains no guarantee, cure, superiority claim, fabricated result or generated before/after; any identifiable patient or testimonial has documented consent and is reviewed before export. Price/offer promotion is blocked or routed to the clinic's documented approval path.
Bad (domain expert would flag): “Guaranteed cure,” unlicensed practitioner presentation, promotional clinical pricing without the required approval, patient footage/health information without permission, or a synthetic transformation represented as a treatment result.
Stakes: Critical
Source: Kuwait Law No. 70 of 2020, Articles 15–16, on health-service advertising/pricing approvals, professional ethics and patient privacy; MovPrompt clinic rules in `packages/creative-engine/src/catalog.ts` and `quality-engine.ts`. This is a product guardrail, not legal advice; launch policy requires Kuwait legal review.

### Known Failure Modes in This Domain

1. **Commercial lookalike failure:** The render appears premium but substitutes a generic product, changes a label/colour, or adds a feature. This is a misrepresentation risk even when a general visual-quality score is high.
2. **RTL conversion failure:** Arabic and English copy, KWD figures, phone numbers or WhatsApp CTA render in the wrong visual order, overlap the subject, or become unreadable after ratio cropping. The asset then fails its conversion purpose despite a valid video file.
3. **Generated proof presented as real proof:** A testimonial, person, clinic result, service outcome, or before/after is fabricated, altered or insufficiently consented. Clinic content adds patient-identifiability and health-claim risks.
4. **Wrongly accepted technical success:** A provider's “completed” candidate is accepted before its final private asset is decoded, inspected, and tied to immutable facts; a later broken link, silent media defect, or reviewer gap becomes the customer's paid result.

### Regulatory / Compliance Context

- Kuwait Law No. 70 of 2020 (Articles 15–16) restricts advertising of health services, prices, products and equipment without competent Ministry approval; it also requires professional ethics and patient privacy. Clinic and pharmacy-adjacent templates must therefore require legal/compliance approval before publication, especially where price, discount, medical/beauty claim, practitioner, patient or health product is involved. [Law No. 70 of 2020](https://www.amr.gov.kw/law70-2020)
- Kuwait's Ministry of Health announced additional 2025 controls for pharmacy and health-product advertising, including prior licensing, accuracy, restrictions on prices/offers and patient/professional privacy. Treat the exact current approval workflow as a launch-time legal verification item rather than encoding a static legal conclusion. [KUNA report on the Ministry decision](https://www.kuna.net.kw/ArticleDetails.aspx?id=3214569&language=ar)
- Arabic and bilingual campaign rendering must preserve RTL direction and the left-to-right handling of numbers and Latin-script runs. This is a rendering-quality requirement, supported by the [W3C Arabic and Persian Layout Requirements](https://www.w3.org/TR/alreq/), rather than a substitute for localized copy review.
- Retail, ecommerce and salon content still requires truth, consent and consumer-safe promotion review, but no additional Kuwait sector-specific rule was relied on for this phase. New verticals, endorsements, financial claims, supplements, minors, or regulated products require a separate legal/policy review before launch.

## 2. Framework Decision

**Selected Framework:** Existing direct TypeScript durable-worker composition: PostgreSQL transactional outbox + pg-boss + Vercel AI SDK 7 inside the existing provider adapter

**Version:** PostgreSQL 17; pg-boss 12.26.x; AI SDK 7; current repository-pinned TypeScript workspaces

**Rationale:**
Phase 4 is a deterministic, single-operation content-generation pipeline, not a conversational or multi-agent system. The repository already has immutable render and attempt records, ledger settlement, transactional outbox scheduling, pg-boss execution, a server-only capability registry, provider adapters, FFmpeg/FFprobe acceptance and private S3-compatible storage. A second orchestration framework would duplicate state, retry and observability boundaries and weaken the existing exactly-once design.

**Alternatives Considered:**

| Framework | Ruled Out Because |
|-----------|------------------|
| LangGraph TypeScript | Checkpointed graphs add a second workflow state model without improving this linear, explicit database lifecycle. Reconsider only for future genuinely branching human-in-the-loop AI workflows. |
| LangChain / CrewAI | Agent abstractions do not fit deterministic video submission, media validation and financial settlement. |

**Vendor Lock-In Accepted:** Partial — provider-specific video transport stays behind the existing server-only adapter and semantic capability registry.

### Minimal durable entry-point pattern

The following is the desired composition pattern, not a new parallel implementation. It makes the durable database record—not a `Promise`, browser tab, or Gateway response—the source of truth.

```ts
// apps/worker/src/main.ts — existing composition pattern
const generationHandler = createGenerationLifecycleHandler({
  store: createDatabaseRenderLifecycleStore(database.db),
  billing: createDatabaseGenerationBilling(database.db),
  capabilityRegistry,
  adapterRegistry,
  outputPersister,
  outputQualityReviewer,
  reconciliationDelaySeconds: config.renderReconciliationDelaySeconds,

    await worker.enqueueGeneration(payload, {
      singletonKey: `reconcile:${payload.renderRunId}`,
      singletonNextSlot: true,
      delaySeconds,
    });
  },
});

const worker = new PgBossWorker({
  config,
  handlers: { health: createHealthJobHandler(), generation: generationHandler },
});
await worker.start();
```

pg-boss documents worker heartbeats, bounded retries and singleton/debounce behaviour. A duplicate queue delivery is expected, so it must be harmless after the database run/attempt locks are applied; it is never the only duplicate-prevention control. [pg-boss jobs API](https://github.com/timgit/pg-boss/blob/master/docs/api/jobs.md)

### Phase-specific pitfalls

1. **Using `experimental_generateVideo` as the customer lifecycle.** It can take minutes and is expressly experimental. Calling it from a request handler hides the Gateway operation ID and ties recovery to a process lifetime. Keep it limited to explicit smoke tooling; use the persisted start/status adapter for customer runs.
2. **Submitting after a timeout or invalid start response.** Remote acceptance may already have succeeded. Reconcile the same idempotency key/opaque operation; never create a second customer attempt merely because the start response was lost.
3. **Letting pg-boss retries mean provider retries.** pg-boss may redeliver work after heartbeat/expiry. Database state must decide whether the job polls, persists output, reviews, or starts the next authorized quality attempt. Queue retry count is not the customer retry budget.
4. **Trusting a completed output URL.** A provider URL is untrusted, expiring capability data. Enforce exact host, HTTPS, public-address/DNS checks, redirect revalidation, byte limits and content checks before storage; never persist or return that URL.
5. **Approving free-form model prose.** A high-level score without every required dimension/evidence can approve a wrong product, Arabic copy or clinic claim. Parse strict Zod JSON, reject missing/duplicate dimensions, and fail closed when the reviewer cannot produce a contract-valid result.

## 4. Implementation Guidance

### Model and runtime policy

Use `bytedance/seedance-2.5` only inside the server-only `video.cinematic` and `video.product_fidelity` adapters once the capability is live-canary approved. Seedance v1 Pro Fast is development smoke tooling only; Omni remains unavailable. The browser receives only capability aliases and sanitized state.

Use a separately configured multimodal language model through AI Gateway only to review a candidate already copied to private MovPrompt storage. It must be restricted to the approved reviewer-model allowlist in worker configuration, with `temperature: 0`, a 120-second default deadline (180 seconds maximum), explicit media-byte caps and no tools. Do not make the reviewer decide charges, provider retries, project acceptance pointers or access control—those remain deterministic PostgreSQL/worker policy decisions.

### Core render pattern

```ts
// apps/worker/src/main.ts — the exact existing worker composition to retain
const generationHandler = createGenerationLifecycleHandler({
  store: createDatabaseRenderLifecycleStore(database.db),
  billing: createDatabaseGenerationBilling(database.db),
  capabilityRegistry,
  adapterRegistry,
  ...(outputPersister ? { outputPersister } : {}),
  ...(outputQualityReviewer ? { outputQualityReviewer } : {}),
  reconciliationDelaySeconds: config.renderReconciliationDelaySeconds,
  scheduleReconciliation: async (payload, delaySeconds) => {
    const currentWorker = await workerReady;
    await currentWorker.enqueueGeneration(payload, {
      singletonKey: `reconcile:${payload.renderRunId}`,
      singletonNextSlot: true,
      delaySeconds,
    });
  },
});
```

`createGenerationLifecycleHandler` already owns the durable branch structure: an existing provider operation is reconciled; a new operation receives `provider.submit:${runId}:${qualityAttempt}`; provider identity is stored through `recordProviderSubmission`; provider acceptance is finalized through the idempotent domain service; completed provider output passes the persister and reviewer before `store.complete`; a quality retry invokes the immutable `prepareQualityRetry` path. Phase work must close gaps inside this function rather than introduce a second `advance*` lifecycle.

### Tool and provider configuration

1. Resolve only a semantic capability server-side. Reject a request with a raw provider/model ID before reaching the adapter.
2. Submit Seedance with a stable `provider.submit:${runId}:${qualityAttempt}` idempotency key. Persist the returned opaque operation with `recordProviderSubmission` before the next status poll or customer-visible processing stage.
3. For `4:5`, request the supported source canvas and perform deterministic 4:5 crop/normalization before technical acceptance. The quote's ratio remains immutable; do not silently downgrade or relabel the output.
4. Treat an unknown host, missing output URL, malformed status body, ambiguous start response or transient network failure as a reconcile/retryable internal state. Do not create another provider operation.
5. The output persister runs before quality review: exact reviewed host → HTTPS/public-address and redirect checks → bounded fetch → MP4 magic/MIME → FFmpeg normalization → FFprobe → full decode → private object key plus hash/media metadata.
6. Give the reviewer a short-lived signed URL only long enough to download bounded bytes into worker memory. Do not log, store, return or place signed/provider URLs in prompts, evidence or public API fields.

### State, recovery, and context strategy

PostgreSQL is the full workflow state. `render_runs` holds the public state/stage and immutable quote binding; `render_attempts` holds candidate, provider telemetry and quality evidence; the ledger/entitlement tables hold exact economic settlement. pg-boss only schedules work and sends heartbeats. It must be possible to delete a worker process after provider acceptance and resume by loading the same persisted run and operation.

The quality model receives deliberately bounded context, not an unbounded project dump:

- confirmed `creativeBrief`, normalized and capped at 24,000 characters;
- at most five verified reference images and one stored candidate video, each capped by worker byte limits;
- the fixed dimension/rubric prompt and no prior reviewer chat history;
- model output restricted to scores plus concise evidence, parsed with a strict schema.

This prevents old drafts, unconfirmed imported claims, signed URLs, OAuth tokens and raw Gateway payloads from leaking into reviewer context. It also makes a retry independently reviewable and reproducible from the immutable project version.

### Error and retry policy

| Condition | Worker action | Customer/economic result |
|---|---|---|
| Gateway status pending or network ambiguity | Save retryable state and schedule reconciliation of the same operation | No duplicate submission, no additional charge |
| Candidate URL/host/media acquisition fails | Preserve evidence; reconcile same operation where possible, otherwise reject candidate | Not completed; quality retry only within the existing quote budget |
| FFprobe/full decode fails | Mark candidate invalid and use the bounded internal retry path | Last accepted version unchanged |
| Reviewer timeout, schema failure, missing dimension or ambiguous decision | Fail closed; make it retryable only if policy has remaining same-config attempts | Never auto-approve; no added customer cost |
| Quality rejection with attempts left | Persist decision, target retry directive and create next same-config attempt | One quote still covers the work |
| Retry budget exhausted / confirmed terminal provider failure | Mark failed and call the idempotent release/refund service once | Honest failure; project/version preserved for manual retry |
| Cancellation after provider acceptance | Remain `cancelling` while adapter reconciles authoritative terminal state | Refund only after confirmed provider cancellation or terminal failure |

### What not to expose

The public API can expose `runId`, semantic capability, public stage, retryability, sanitized error code, accepted output metadata and a fresh owner-authorized download URL. It must never expose Seedance/Gateway model IDs, opaque provider operations, provider error body, raw reviewer response, quality-model name, provider URL, signed URL persisted in records, or cost telemetry beyond a product-approved customer quote.

## 4b. AI Systems Best Practices

> Written by `gsd-ai-researcher`. Cross-cutting patterns every developer building AI systems needs — independent of framework choice.

### 4b.1 Structured outputs with Zod (and offline Pydantic interoperability)

The canonical production contract is Zod because the worker is TypeScript. Every AI quality response must parse exactly, contain all required dimensions, and be sent through the deterministic `evaluateAcceptedOutput` policy. Do not parse model prose or coerce absent values.

```ts
// apps/worker/src/media-quality-analyzers.ts
import { generateText, Output } from "ai";
import { z } from "zod";

const ScoreSchema = z.number().int().min(0).max(100);
const EvidenceSchema = z.string().trim().min(1).max(500);

export const QualityJudgeSchema = z.object({
  scores: z.object({
    product_identity: ScoreSchema,
    prompt_adherence: ScoreSchema,
    motion_realism: ScoreSchema,
    visual_artifacts: ScoreSchema,
    brand_safety: ScoreSchema,
    dialect_fidelity: ScoreSchema,
    speech_sync: ScoreSchema,
    safe_zones: ScoreSchema,
    compliance: ScoreSchema,
  }).strict(),
  evidence: z.object({
    product_identity: EvidenceSchema,
    prompt_adherence: EvidenceSchema,
    motion_realism: EvidenceSchema,
    visual_artifacts: EvidenceSchema,
    brand_safety: EvidenceSchema,
    dialect_fidelity: EvidenceSchema,
    speech_sync: EvidenceSchema,
    safe_zones: EvidenceSchema,
    compliance: EvidenceSchema,
  }).strict(),
}).strict();

const result = await generateText({
  model: languageModel,
  messages: [{ role: "user", content: boundedCandidateAndBrief }],
  output: Output.object({
    name: "movprompt_video_quality_review",
    description: "Scores and timestamped evidence for every acceptance dimension.",
    schema: QualityJudgeSchema,
  }),
  temperature: 0,
  abortSignal: AbortSignal.timeout(120_000),
});
const judgment = QualityJudgeSchema.parse(result.output);
```

The small Pydantic mirror below is **not** a production service dependency. Offline Python evaluators may use it when labeling or inspecting a benchmark corpus; it mirrors the public schema so independent research tooling cannot quietly accept a different shape.

```python
# Offline evaluator interoperability only; do not add Pydantic to the Node worker.
from pydantic import BaseModel, ConfigDict, Field

class QualityScores(BaseModel):
    model_config = ConfigDict(extra="forbid")
    product_identity: int = Field(ge=0, le=100)
    prompt_adherence: int = Field(ge=0, le=100)
    motion_realism: int = Field(ge=0, le=100)
    visual_artifacts: int = Field(ge=0, le=100)
    brand_safety: int = Field(ge=0, le=100)
    dialect_fidelity: int = Field(ge=0, le=100)
    speech_sync: int = Field(ge=0, le=100)
    safe_zones: int = Field(ge=0, le=100)
    compliance: int = Field(ge=0, le=100)

class QualityJudgeResult(BaseModel):
    model_config = ConfigDict(extra="forbid")
    scores: QualityScores
    evidence: dict[str, str]
```

Retry structured reviewer calls at most once for a clearly retryable transport failure before recording a fail-closed quality observation. Do not retry schema-invalid/ambiguous output with altered facts or loosened prompts. Log only `runId`, attempt number, evaluator version, sanitized failure code, latency and token/cost metadata; keep raw model output out of user-visible logs. Surface an honest retryable processing state or failed result only after the lifecycle policy decides the bounded internal generation retry/settlement path.

### 4b.2 Async-first design

The production render path is async by design: HTTP creates durable state, pg-boss delivers jobs, the adapter starts/polls remote operations, storage/FFmpeg work uses awaited I/O, and reviewer calls are awaited only inside the worker. Use `await` for every state-changing boundary so a step is recorded before the next one begins.

The common mistake is keeping a long-lived `generateVideo()` promise or `setInterval` poll in an API handler/browser and calling `asyncio.run()`-style nested event-loop work in a test harness. In Node, never block the event loop with synchronous media work or create detached promises that can submit after a cancellation. Spawn FFmpeg through the existing bounded `execFile` seam, propagate `AbortSignal`, and make a job retry return to persisted state.

Streaming is not a customer correctness mechanism here. Use browser polling/subscriptions only for progress UX; use awaited `generateText` plus `Output.object` for quality review because acceptance needs a complete schema-valid response. Video data and final files should never stream through the browser on the critical path.

### 4b.3 Prompt engineering discipline

- Keep immutable system/rubric instructions in `qualityPrompt`; pass the confirmed brief, referenced assets and candidate as distinct user content. User/imported copy is evidence to evaluate, never higher-priority instructions.
- Do not concatenate unbounded product-page HTML, provider errors, OCR output or previous reviewer prose into the prompt. Strip/normalize/cap the confirmed brief and use selected verified references only.
- Use few-shot examples only when benchmark evidence shows a specific scoring calibration benefit. Store them as fixed, reviewed evaluator assets; never dynamically retrieve unreviewed customer media/examples into the system prompt.
- Set explicit bounds: temperature `0`, quality request timeout `120_000` ms (hard maximum `180_000` ms), five reference images, 24,000 brief characters, 18 MiB candidate video, 8 MiB total references, evidence strings ≤500 characters. AI SDK text-token limits must also be set explicitly when the approved reviewer model/configuration supports them; no production reviewer call may use an unbounded output budget.

### 4b.4 Context-window management

This is a single-turn judge, not conversational memory. Do not carry previous attempts' prompts, candidate frames or reviewer outputs forward. Each attempt builds a fresh deterministic context from the immutable configuration:

1. Select only references that establish product/brand identity; cap at five and order deterministically.
2. Canonicalize the confirmed brief, include critical facts (product identity, price, offer, CTA, language, vertical policy, safe zones), and truncate before model context is approached.
3. If the input exceeds a hard cap, reject the review as `quality_context_unsupported` rather than silently dropping business facts. A later human/admin workflow may repair the project; automatic acceptance may not.
4. Save concise structured evidence and evaluator version in the attempt record, not chat history. The next generation attempt receives a deterministic retry directive derived from failed dimensions, never raw model chain-of-thought or untrusted output.

### 4b.5 Cost and latency budget

The customer quote covers one accepted output, so budget all internal cost per accepted output: Seedance initial attempt + up to two quality retries + technical processing + one reviewer call per candidate. Capture Gateway cost as integer micro-USD and worker latency in the attempt record; calculate acceptance-adjusted cost from real canary data, not a client constant.

Use a three-tier policy:

| Work | Cost/latency policy |
|---|---|
| Deterministic validation | Zod, FFprobe, full decode, hashes and policy code first; no model call. |
| Candidate review | One bounded, low-temperature multimodal call after technical success only; no review for media that already fails technical gates. |
| Retried render | At most two same-configuration internal attempts, only when a persisted quality directive/policy says it may improve the result. |

Do not semantic-cache a quality decision across different candidate bytes. Exact caching is allowed only for immutable technical results keyed by object SHA-256 and analyzer version, and for identical quote/configuration hashes before a render begins. Any cache hit must retain the original evidence/version and still obey ownership boundaries. Alert on p95 Gateway/reviewer latency, candidate-download failure rate, reviewer schema-invalid rate, cost per accepted output and retry-budget exhaustion; these are signals to pause a capability, not to silently select a weaker provider.

## 5. Evaluation Strategy

### 5.1 Evaluation dimensions and release rubrics

Phase 04 is a content-generation system with an autonomous durable worker. Evaluation therefore combines content accuracy and publishability with safety, task completion, tool use, recovery and economic correctness. Deterministic checks run before any subjective judge. A multimodal judge may support visual acceptance only after it is calibrated against human labels; it never overrides a deterministic hard failure.

| Dimension | Priority | Release rubric |
|-----------|----------|----------------|
| Durable task completion and same-operation recovery | Critical | **PASS:** A submitted run persists one provider operation, survives browser closure and worker/process restart, resumes that same operation, and reaches exactly one honest terminal state. The accepted project pointer advances only after private technical and quality acceptance. **FAIL:** A timeout/restart creates another provider request, browser polling controls completion, a run stays orphaned, or a failed regeneration replaces the previous accepted version. **Measurement:** Code — worker/pg-boss fixture plus disposable-PostgreSQL race test; Human — one explicitly authorized canary recovery walkthrough. |
| Output origin, ownership and technical media validity | Critical | **PASS:** Every accepted result came through an exact reviewed HTTPS host with redirect and public-address revalidation, was copied to a MovPrompt private object, has an SHA-256 checksum, and its **final normalized object** fully decodes as MP4/H.264 with the quoted ratio, minimum short side (470 for 480p; 700 for 720p), duration within 1.25 seconds of the brief and audio presence matching policy. **FAIL:** A provider URL is retained as the deliverable, a host/redirect/address is unapproved, the stored object is missing, only the input was probed, or final codec/dimensions/duration/audio/full-decode checks fail. **Measurement:** Code — URL/network fixtures, object metadata, FFprobe and full FFmpeg decode. |
| Product identity and confirmed business facts | Critical | **PASS:** The product/service, variant, packaging, label, logo, colour and all visible/spoken name, price, offer, CTA and destination agree with the immutable confirmed brief; exact price/offer/CTA accuracy is 100%, and `product_identity` is at least 90. **FAIL:** Any unconfirmed feature, result, price, discount, CTA, booking/WhatsApp destination, product variant or service claim appears, even when the video is visually attractive. **Measurement:** Code — immutable configuration/hash and exact deterministic-field comparison; Calibrated multimodal judge — reference adherence and timecoded evidence; Human — brand-owner/merchandiser adjudication. |
| Kuwait Arabic and bilingual integrity | High | **PASS:** Requested ar-KW or bilingual copy is readable, correctly joined and ordered; Arabic, English, KWD figures and phone/WhatsApp runs keep the intended bidi order; spoken Arabic uses the approved Kuwait register; `dialect_fidelity` is at least 90. **FAIL:** Disconnected/reversed Arabic, wrong dialect/register, clipped translation, reversed numbers, unreadable subtitles or speech that changes the approved line. English-only outputs receive a not-applicable score of 100 only when no Arabic was requested. **Measurement:** Code — language/dialect contract and expected-string inventory; Calibrated multimodal judge — visual/speech evidence; Human — Kuwait Arabic copywriter review. |
| Conversion legibility and safe zones | High | **PASS:** Product, logo, price, subtitles and CTA remain within the quoted ratio's protected layout, are unobstructed and readable for their intended scene duration; all critical price/CTA/end-card frames pass, and `safe_zones` is at least 75. **FAIL:** Cropping, a subject, generated decoration or captions hide/collide with a protected layer, or the next action cannot be read before the scene changes. **Measurement:** Code — final canvas/ratio and template safe-zone metadata; Calibrated multimodal judge — sampled-frame overlap/legibility evidence; Human — social creative review at delivery size. |
| Motion, artifact and publishable visual quality | High | **PASS:** Motion, hands, faces, reflections, product geometry, camera moves and visible speech remain coherent; no material flicker, morphing, broken geometry or identity drift is present; `motion_realism` and `visual_artifacts` are each at least 75 and a creative reviewer rates publishability at least 4/5. **FAIL:** Temporal defects, implausible handling, warped product/person, unsynchronised visible speech, static-placeholder output or an artifact that would prevent publishing. **Measurement:** Calibrated multimodal judge with timestamped evidence; Human — media QA/creative director, including slow-motion/frame inspection for disagreements. |
| Template, goal, tone and creative usefulness | High | **PASS:** The complete story follows the selected template/goal, requested tone and campaign action without copying an unrelated sample; `prompt_adherence` and `brand_safety` are at least 75 and the result is independently publishable rather than a demo placeholder. **FAIL:** The wrong outcome/template story is rendered, the source product/service is replaced by demo media, the requested tone is contradicted, or the result is materially repetitive/unusable despite passing file checks. **Measurement:** Calibrated multimodal judge; Human — performance marketer/brand reviewer. |
| User-facing safety and regulated-content compliance | Critical | **PASS:** Only confirmed claims and consented people/assets appear; clinic content contains no guarantee, cure, fabricated result, deceptive generated before/after, unapproved patient identity or unauthorized price/offer, and all required clinic-review states block acceptance until cleared. **FAIL:** Any unsafe/unconfirmed claim, deceptive proof, patient/privacy exposure or missing required approval is accepted. Critical-safety false acceptance must be 0%. **Measurement:** Code — preflight/policy/consent rules; Calibrated multimodal judge — visible/spoken claim detection; Human — 100% licensed-clinic/compliance review during Phase 04 calibration and before clinic production enablement. |
| Bounded retries and exact settlement | Critical | **PASS:** One quote produces one initial attempt plus at most two internal quality retries, one provider acceptance settlement, and exactly one final charge/entitlement outcome; terminal failure or confirmed cancellation restores value once. **FAIL:** A retry changes immutable facts/configuration, creates an extra customer charge, exceeds the retry budget, or concurrent delivery/cancel/reconcile paths double-charge, double-refund or leave value reserved. **Measurement:** Code — PostgreSQL concurrency, ledger uniqueness/idempotency and lifecycle property tests. |
| Cancellation and recovery truth | Critical | **PASS:** Pre-acceptance cancellation releases immediately; post-acceptance cancellation remains `cancelling` until the provider confirms a terminal state; a missing output or uncertain state reconciles the same operation; the previous accepted result remains downloadable. **FAIL:** The UI/API reports cancelled/refunded before confirmation, resubmits an uncertain operation, or loses the prior accepted output. **Measurement:** Code — API/worker state-machine tests plus browser fixture; Human — canary cancellation walkthrough when a proven provider contract permits it. |
| Public boundary, ownership and refreshed download | Critical | **PASS:** Only the owner can obtain a fresh short-lived URL for an accepted private object; expired URLs refresh successfully; public DTOs/logs expose only safe stages/codes/request IDs and contain no provider/model IDs, opaque operation IDs, raw errors, secrets, object keys, signed/provider URLs, reviewer internals or retry counts. **FAIL:** Cross-user access succeeds, an expired URL becomes permanent failure, or any forbidden field leaks. **Measurement:** Code — owner/RLS/storage tests, response-schema snapshots, structured-log redaction and secret-canary scans. |

The production acceptance policy remains fail-closed: all hard gates pass, the weighted quality score is at least 85, `product_identity` and applicable `dialect_fidelity` are at least 90, and every other scored production dimension is at least 75. These thresholds are configuration/version controlled and may become stricter after calibration; they may not be lowered to make a canary pass.

### 5.2 Multimodal-judge calibration

- The judge is evaluated against human labels, never against its own prior scores. Two qualified reviewers independently label each calibration candidate; disagreements are adjudicated by the domain role named in Section 1b. Clinic/compliance labels require a licensed clinic representative or Kuwait healthcare advertising/privacy reviewer.
- Before the judge may approve production output, binary accept/reject agreement must reach weighted Cohen's kappa `>= 0.70`, per-dimension rank correlation with adjudicated human scores must reach Spearman `>= 0.70`, and false acceptance on critical product/fact/compliance defects must be `0` in the locked set.
- A schema-invalid, missing-dimension, timed-out, unsupported or uncalibrated judge result is not a low score and is never an approval. It becomes a fail-closed retryable observation or human-review state according to the lifecycle policy.
- Calibration is repeated whenever the reviewer model, prompt, schema, quality thresholds, template policy or media preprocessing version changes. The evaluator version and rubric version are stored with every decision.
- `apps/worker/src/benchmark-manifest.ts` is the executable calibration contract. Its pure functions validate the locked labels, calculate the binary confusion matrix, weighted Cohen's kappa, tie-aware per-dimension Spearman correlations and critical false-accept count, then derive `passed` from the thresholds above. `apps/worker/src/benchmark-manifest.test.ts` recomputes every stored metric and fails on missing/duplicate labels, non-independent reviewer roles, absent adjudication, inadequate per-dimension counts, version mismatch or metric drift.
- `apps/worker/src/__fixtures__/kw-video-48-v1.calibration.json` is the versioned sanitized evidence artifact. It records dataset/evaluator/rubric versions, two independent reviewer role IDs and labels per case, adjudications, confusion/correlation counts, thresholds and derived pass result. It contains no media/provider/signed URL, provider operation, secret, raw media, raw reviewer prose or personal reviewer identity.
- `createOutputQualityReviewer` must load and validate that artifact against the active evaluator/rubric versions. It cannot return `accepted`, and worker quality readiness cannot advertise automatic acceptance, unless the artifact is complete, exact-version and passing. Missing, stale, failed or mismatched evidence yields fail-closed `needs_review`/quality-unavailable state while persisted operations remain recoverable.

### 5.3 Reference dataset

**Dataset:** `kw-video-48-v1`, built from the existing `PROVIDER_BENCHMARK_CORPUS` and facts-locked `kw-48-assets-v1` manifest.

| Property | Specification |
|----------|---------------|
| Size | 48 immutable briefs before production activation: 4 verticals (salon, clinic, retail, ecommerce) × 3 language modes (English, Arabic, bilingual) × 4 challenges (identity, motion, Arabic, conversion). Each challenge deterministically covers one delivery ratio, so every vertical/language combination covers 9:16, 1:1, 4:5 and 16:9. |
| Assets | Sixteen rights-attested subject/reference packs reused consistently across language variants. Store checksum, MIME, dimensions and private object key; never store a signed URL in the manifest. |
| Labels | Immutable confirmed facts and configuration hash; expected product/variant/price/offer/CTA/destination; language/dialect; ratio/resolution/audio; safe-zone map; consent/compliance expectation; expected technical outcome; two independent role-qualified human accept/reject labels and 0–100 dimension scores; role-qualified adjudication; timestamped sanitized evidence and defect category. |
| Composition | At least 24 expected-pass and 24 expected-fail candidates. Failure coverage includes corrupt/truncated MP4, wrong codec/ratio/duration/audio, disallowed/redirected host, product/label drift, altered price/CTA, disconnected or wrong-dialect Arabic, clipped bilingual copy, safe-zone collision, flicker/warped hands, invented testimonial/clinic claim, missing consent and leaked public diagnostics. |
| Labeling | Two independent domain reviewers plus adjudication. Media QA owns technical labels; Kuwait Arabic reviewer owns dialect/bidi labels; brand owner/merchandiser owns product/fact labels; creative director owns motion/safe-zone/publishability; clinic specialist owns regulated cases. Automated labels are permitted only for deterministic file/configuration facts. |
| Creation timeline | Start in Wave 0 with non-billable checked-in/private fixtures and historical approved/rejected media. Materialize one labeled candidate per brief during implementation. An external Seedance sample is added only under a separately authorized paid-canary checklist; CI never generates paid media. Production failures become new redacted regression fixtures after rights/privacy review. |

No provider comparison is considered complete unless all 48 brief IDs are present exactly once with the same manifest checksums. A small authorized canary can prove runtime wiring, but it cannot replace the 48-brief quality reference set.

### 5.4 Tooling and CI/CD integration

No eval/tracing product is currently detected, and Phase 04 research explicitly found no need for a new dependency. The implementation default is therefore the repository's existing stack:

- Vitest for deterministic regression, orchestration and schema tests;
- FFmpeg/FFprobe plus immutable media fixtures for technical evaluation;
- Zod for strict reviewer output and benchmark-record validation;
- PostgreSQL render/attempt/ledger/audit records plus structured request/run/job logs for trace reconstruction;
- the existing 48-brief benchmark and human calibration sheets for subjective evaluation.

Install/restore the approved toolchain with:

```bash
bun install --frozen-lockfile
```

Arize Phoenix is **not** a Phase 04 runtime dependency because introducing a second telemetry store conflicts with the no-new-dependency and private-media constraints. It may later be used as an optional offline viewer by exporting redacted OpenTelemetry spans from non-production evaluation data; raw customer media, signed URLs, provider payloads and secrets must never be exported. Promptfoo is likewise unnecessary for this non-conversational pipeline while Vitest already owns the prompt/schema regression boundary.

Wave 0 must add a single non-billable `bun run eval:phase04` script that executes the following gates (or their exact workspace equivalents) and fails on any skipped required fixture:

```bash
bun run --cwd packages/creative-engine test --run src/creative-engine.test.ts src/types.test.ts
bun run --cwd packages/providers test --run src/vercel-gateway-seedance.test.ts
bun run --cwd apps/worker test --run src/benchmark-manifest.test.ts src/live-benchmark.test.ts src/render-lifecycle.test.ts src/output-persister.test.ts src/output-quality-reviewer.test.ts src/media-quality-analyzers.test.ts
bun run --cwd apps/api test --run src/generation.test.ts src/generation-service.test.ts
bun run --cwd packages/db test --run test/generation-service.test.ts
# In the disposable PostgreSQL 17 CI job:
bun run --cwd packages/db test --run test/generation-service.postgres.test.ts
```

The phase gate additionally requires `bun run test:all && bun run typecheck && bun run build:all`, the Phase 04 redaction verifier, full-decode fixture evidence, browser evidence for pending cancellation and expired-download refresh, and one separately budget-authorized Seedance 2.5 canary. Automated test, build, migration and eval commands must never trigger a paid provider call.

The paid canary has two calibration checkpoints: before submission and again before candidate acceptance, the runbook recomputes the versioned artifact, checks all 48 independently labeled/adjudicated cases, matches the active evaluator/rubric versions and requires the stored thresholds/pass result. A canary is additional transport/storage/settlement evidence and cannot create, replace or waive the calibration set.

## 6. Guardrails

### 6.1 Online guardrails (run on every relevant request/candidate)

| Guardrail | Classification and trigger | Enforcement/action | Latency policy |
|-----------|----------------------------|--------------------|----------------|
| One operation, bounded attempts and exact settlement | Catastrophic financial/task-completion risk. Runs at quote/start, submit, queue redelivery, retry, cancellation and terminal settlement. | PostgreSQL owner transaction, unique idempotency keys, advisory/row locks, persisted provider operation and max two internal retries. Unknown submission state parks/reconciles the same operation. Duplicate or contradictory transition is rejected; no second provider call or ledger mutation. | Database-only and synchronous at each state boundary. |
| Safe private technical acceptance | Catastrophic SSRF/data-loss/broken-delivery risk. Runs for every provider candidate before visual review. | Exact reviewed host, HTTPS, public-address/DNS pinning and redirect revalidation; bounded bytes and MP4 signature; normalize; write private object; SHA-256; probe and full decode the final object. Any failure rejects/parks the candidate and never marks the run complete. | Deterministic checks first; no multimodal call for a technically invalid candidate. |
| Fact, identity, language and compliance acceptance | Catastrophic misrepresentation/safety risk. Runs for every technically valid candidate. | Compare against immutable confirmed facts/reference assets, require complete schema-valid multimodal evidence and production thresholds, and apply clinic/consent policy. A missing/ambiguous reviewer result fails closed. Failed dimensions create only a bounded same-config retry directive; exhaustion fails/refunds rather than accepting the best rejected candidate. | One bounded judge call after technical success; 120-second target, 180-second hard maximum. |
| Owner-only output and public redaction | Catastrophic privacy/security risk. Runs on status, logs, download refresh and support responses. | Owner/RLS check before signing; short-lived response URL only; response schema allowlist; structured logger sanitization. | Runtime readiness and kill switch | Catastrophic partial-system activation risk. Runs before every new quote/submission. | Fail closed unless API/worker fingerprints, heartbeat, approved capability, pricing, private storage, FFmpeg/FFprobe and reviewer agree. ### 6.2 Offline quality flywheel

| Signal | Batch and review cadence | Improvement action |
|--------|--------------------------|--------------------|
| Judge-human disagreement and critical false accepts | Weekly during private beta; monthly only after three consecutive passing cycles. Review all disagreements and all critical product/fact/compliance cases. | Re-label/adjudicate, add the case to `kw-video-48-v1`, adjust rubric/prompt or route the dimension to human review. Never lower a critical threshold merely to improve agreement. |
| Language/template/ratio quality drift | Weekly stratified sample across vertical, English/Arabic/bilingual, template and ratio; include every retry/exhaustion and near-threshold acceptance. | Add failure fixture, improve template/prompt/safe-zone policy, or pause the affected capability/template/language slice. |

| Cost, latency and accepted-output yield | Daily aggregation by capability/configuration, never by public provider ID. | Investigate retry causes and candidate invalidity; pause new submissions if contribution margin or reliability leaves the approved envelope. No silent model-family downgrade. |
| User signal versus metric divergence | Monthly comparison of quality score with downloads, manual retries, project abandonment and support complaints. | Treat divergence as a missing rubric/failure mode; human-review examples and extend the reference set before changing acceptance logic. |

Every flywheel sample is owner/privacy scoped. Reviewers receive private, time-bounded access; exports contain only approved redacted metadata and dataset IDs. Clinic/patient media is excluded from general sampling unless its consent and evaluation purpose explicitly permit review.

## 7. Production Monitoring

### 7.1 Trace and evidence system

The production tracing system is the existing structured telemetry, not a new vendor:

```text
requestId -> projectVersionId -> renderRunId -> qualityAttempt -> pg-boss job/worker
          -> private object SHA-256 -> technical analyzer version
          -> quality evaluator/rubric version -> ledger/audit transition
```

PostgreSQL `render_runs`, immutable `render_attempts`, ledger/entitlement entries and `audit_logs` provide the durable trace; API/worker structured logs provide timing and operational context. Every event includes the available `requestId`, `runId`, `attemptNumber`, `jobId` and `workerId`. Internal secure records may retain the opaque provider operation needed for reconciliation, but public responses and normal logs never include it. Signed URLs, object bodies/keys, raw prompts/provider payloads, OAuth tokens, secrets and raw reviewer output are excluded. Phoenix remains an optional future **offline redacted evaluation viewer**, not production truth or a runtime dependency.

### 7.2 Metrics and alert thresholds

| Metric | Target / alert |
|--------|----------------|
| Duplicate provider operation, customer charge, entitlement consumption or refund | Target `0`. Page immediately on the first invariant/uniqueness violation. |
| Runs accepted without private object checksum, technical pass, complete quality evidence or accepted attempt | Target `0`. Page immediately; disable new submissions through the kill switch. |
| Technical job success | `>= 98%` over the last 24 hours once `n >= 20`; warn below 98%, page below 95% or on three consecutive invalid candidates with the same defect. |
| First-attempt accepted-output rate | `>= 80%` weekly overall and reported by template/language/ratio. Warn below target at `n >= 20`; do not hide low-volume slices. |
| Accepted within one internal retry | `>= 92%` weekly. Alert when retry exhaustion exceeds 8% or any run exceeds the configured two-retry maximum. |
| Exact price, offer, CTA and destination accuracy | `100%` in automated/human samples. Any confirmed mismatch is a critical incident and pauses the affected template/capability. |
| Product identity pass | `>= 90` per accepted run; reference-set pass rate `>= 95%`. Any accepted critical identity swap triggers incident review. |
| Arabic/bilingual and safe-zone pass | Applicable `dialect_fidelity >= 90`; critical price/CTA/end-card safe-zone pass `100%`; all protected-frame pass `>= 99%`. Any accepted disconnected/reversed Arabic or wrong destination is critical. |
| Clinic/compliance false acceptance | Target `0`. Page and disable clinic generation on the first accepted prohibited claim, patient/privacy or consent failure. |
| Judge health/calibration | Schema-invalid `< 0.5%`, timeout `< 1%`, kappa and Spearman each `>= 0.70`; failure of calibration disables automatic approval and routes to fail-closed/manual review. |
| Stuck workflow | Warn when `rendering` has no durable update for `max(2 × configured poll cap, 10 min)`, `securing_output` for 5 min, `quality_review` for 3 min or `cancelling` for 15 min; page at twice those windows or if no reconciliation job exists. |
| Settlement lag | No terminal run remains reserved/unrefunded for more than 5 minutes. Page on any double transition or on a nonzero unsettled terminal count beyond the window. |
| Private output/download refresh | Accepted-output private-object presence `100%`; owner-authorized refresh success `>= 99%` over 24 hours at `n >= 20`; page on cross-user success or stored signed URL detection. |
| Cost and latency | Record integer micro-USD and latency for every available attempt. Missing cost on an accepted attempt `< 1%`; contribution margin per accepted output `>= 60%`; warn on p95 provider/reviewer latency >2× the trailing 7-day baseline. |
| Redaction/secrets | Target `0` forbidden-field or secret-canary matches in public payloads/log streams. Page immediately and revoke/rotate exposed credentials if one appears. |

### 7.3 Sampling and review operations

- **Every request/run/attempt:** collect deterministic lifecycle, technical, settlement, authorization, redaction, cost and latency metrics. Every candidate receives the complete technical gate and structured quality gate; this is acceptance, not sampling.
- **Human review:** review 100% of `needs_review`, retry-exhausted, near-threshold (within 5 points), judge-error and user-complaint cases. During Phase 04/private beta, also review 100% of clinic candidates and Arabic/bilingual accepted outputs until two consecutive 48-case calibration cycles pass.
- **Random accepted sample:** review at least 10% weekly, stratified by vertical, template, language, ratio, resolution and initial/retried attempt. After 500 stable accepted outputs and three passing monthly calibration cycles this may reduce to 5%, but clinic/legal policy may require a higher floor.
- **Reference regression:** run the non-billable deterministic suite on every pull request. Run the full 48-case human/judge calibration before reviewer/prompt/schema/threshold/template-policy changes and monthly in production. Paid regeneration is a separately approved manual experiment, never a CI side effect.
- **Retention and access:** store metric/evidence versions and sanitized codes, not raw signed URLs or provider payloads. Keep sampled media private under the original ownership/consent policy; record reviewer access in audit logs and remove a sample when rights are revoked.

An alert is closed only with database, worker, media and, where relevant, rendered-browser evidence. A green model/provider dashboard alone is not proof that MovPrompt produced or settled an accepted result.
