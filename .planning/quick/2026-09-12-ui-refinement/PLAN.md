# MovPrompt UI refinement

## Intent

Enhance the existing Creator experience without changing the established warm-neutral and amber brand palette, information architecture, routes, or product behavior.

## Scope

- Refine the shared Creator shell and navigation hierarchy.
- Improve template discovery, comparison density, card actions, and responsive behavior.
- Preserve English and Arabic behavior, light and dark themes, and existing accessibility semantics.
- Verify the browser journey at 375, 768, 1024, and 1440 pixels.

## Validation

- Web typecheck, lint, focused tests, and production build.
- Browser checks for templates, creation, and projects in light/dark and English/Arabic.
- Keyboard focus, touch targets, reduced motion, overflow, and browser console review.
